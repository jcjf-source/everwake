# Task 08 — Points of interest and discovery journal

## Objective
Add discoverable landmarks and meaningful interactions.

## Scope
- Place ruins, abandoned camps, chests, shrines, and a cave entrance in suitable terrain.
- Each POI has a distinct marker and interaction behavior.
- Discovery reveals/records the POI and adds a journal entry.
- Chests have loot and an opened state; shrines give a small reward or knowledge; camps offer a small find.
- Avoid generic repeated placement; respect local terrain and spacing.
- A cave may be represented as an entrance/interactable in this task; interior can be deferred.

## Acceptance
At least several POI types can be found and interacted with; each has real outcomes; discovery appears in journal; opened/used state is retained on revisit; interactions consume turns consistently.
